# US Macroeconomic Brief — 2026-06-13

## Executive Summary
The US economy shows signs of moderating growth in mid-2026, with Q1 GDP coming in below trend and the labor market gradually cooling. The Federal Reserve has held rates steady through H1 2026 after its 2024-2025 cutting cycle, with core inflation still above the 2% target. Markets are pricing a potential rate cut in September 2026. Fiscal policy uncertainty and trade tensions add complexity to the outlook.

---

## Key Indicators

### GDP Growth
- **Q1 2026 GDP (final)**: +1.6% annualized (BEA, May 28, 2026)
- Down from +2.4% in Q4 2025; consumer spending slowed to +1.8%
- Business fixed investment: +3.2%; residential investment: -2.1%
- Atlanta Fed GDPNow for Q2 2026 (as of June 10): ~+1.9%

### Labor Market
- **May 2026 Nonfarm Payrolls**: +148,000 (BLS, June 6, 2026)
- **Unemployment Rate**: 4.2% (up from 4.1% in April)
- **Labor Force Participation**: 62.5% (stable)
- **Average Hourly Earnings**: +3.6% YoY (cooling from +3.8%)
- JOLTS job openings (April): 7.8M — declining trend continues

### Inflation
- **May 2026 CPI**: +2.8% YoY (BLS, June 11, 2026)
- **Core CPI**: +3.1% YoY — sticky, driven by shelter (+4.2%) and services
- **May 2026 PCEPI** (April data): +2.5% YoY; Core PCE: +2.7% YoY (BEA, May 30)
- Shelter inflation remains the largest contributor; goods deflation moderating

### Federal Reserve Policy
- **Federal Funds Rate**: 3.75%-4.00% (unchanged since December 2025)
- Fed held rates at May and June 2026 FOMC meetings
- **Dot Plot (March 2026 SEP)**: Median projection of 2 cuts in 2026
- **Fed Balance Sheet**: QT continuing at reduced pace ($25B/month Treasuries)
- Chair Powell: "We need greater confidence on inflation before adjusting policy"
- Market pricing (CME FedWatch, June 12): ~65% probability of September cut

### Fiscal & Debt
- **Federal Deficit (FY2026, first 8 months)**: ~$1.4 trillion (CBO)
- Debt-to-GDP: ~102%
- Debt ceiling suspended through January 2027 (2025 Fiscal Responsibility Act)

### Financial Conditions
- **10-Year Treasury Yield**: 4.28% (June 12, 2026)
- **2-Year Treasury Yield**: 3.92%
- **S&P 500**: ~5,820 (June 12, 2026) — up ~8% YTD
- **USD Index (DXY)**: ~103.5 — moderately weaker on rate cut expectations

---

## Key Themes & US Market Implications

1. **Soft Landing Base Case**: Growth moderating but not contracting; labor market cooling orderly. Supports gradual Fed easing in H2 2026.

2. **Sticky Shelter Inflation**: Remains the key obstacle to faster rate cuts. Multi-family construction boom of 2024-2025 now delivering, which should ease rent pressure by late 2026.

3. **Trade Policy Uncertainty**: Tariff regime on China and select trading partners creates supply chain friction. Importers facing cost pressure; domestic manufacturers seeing modest demand shift.

4. **AI Capex Boom**: Corporate AI infrastructure spending remains a bright spot, supporting GDP via structures and equipment investment. NVIDIA, Microsoft, Google capex plans remain elevated.

5. **Consumer Caution**: Savings rate rising to 4.8% from 3.7% a year ago. Credit card delinquencies ticking up. Middle-income households showing more price sensitivity.
