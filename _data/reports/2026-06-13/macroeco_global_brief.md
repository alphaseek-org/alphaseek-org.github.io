# Global Macroeconomic Brief (ex-US, ex-China) — 2026-06-13

## Executive Summary
Global growth outside the US and China remains uneven. Europe is experiencing a modest recovery led by services, while manufacturing continues to struggle. The ECB has begun a cautious easing cycle. Japan's BOJ is navigating a delicate normalization path. Emerging markets face mixed conditions: commodity exporters benefit from elevated prices, while debt-burdened nations contend with tight global financial conditions.

## Key Themes & Global Market Implications

1. **Divergent Central Bank Paths**: ECB easing vs. BOJ tightening creates FX volatility. EUR/USD range-bound; USD/JPY trending lower. Carry trade unwinding risk.

2. **European Manufacturing Weakness**: Structural headwinds from energy costs and Chinese competition. Auto sector particularly exposed to EV transition and Chinese exports.

3. **Japan's Normalization**: BOJ rate hikes positive for Japanese financials and yen; headwind for export-heavy manufacturers. JGB yields rising (10Y JGB: 1.45%).

4. **Supply Chain Reconfiguration**: ASEAN and India continue to gain manufacturing share as firms diversify from China. Vietnam electronics exports surging.

5. **Commodity Price Moderation**: Oil prices subdued on OPEC+ production increases and demand uncertainty. Industrial metals supported by electrification/energy transition demand.
