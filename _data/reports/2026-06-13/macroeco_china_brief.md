# China Macroeconomic Brief — 2026-06-13

## Executive Summary
China's economy continues its moderate recovery trajectory in mid-2026, with manufacturing PMI holding above the expansion threshold and services activity resilient. The PBOC maintains an accommodative stance with targeted liquidity injections, while CPI remains subdued, giving policymakers room to maneuver. Trade tensions with the US remain a headwind, but domestic consumption and tech self-sufficiency investments provide offsetting support.

---

## Key Indicators

### GDP Growth
- **Q1 2026 GDP**: +5.2% YoY (National Bureau of Statistics, April 2026)
- **2026 Full-Year Target**: ~5.0% (NPC session, March 2026)
- Q1 growth slightly above target, driven by manufacturing investment (+9.1% YoY) and infrastructure spending.

### Manufacturing PMI
- **May 2026 Official Manufacturing PMI**: 50.8 (NBS, May 31, 2026) — second consecutive month above 50
- **May 2026 Caixin Manufacturing PMI**: 51.2 (Caixin/S&P Global, June 1, 2026)
- New export orders sub-index: 49.3, reflecting tariff headwinds; domestic new orders: 51.6

### Services & Composite
- **May 2026 Official Non-Manufacturing PMI**: 51.5 (NBS)
- **May 2026 Caixin Services PMI**: 52.1 (Caixin/S&P Global, June 3, 2026)
- Consumer services (travel, dining) robust; property-related services still contracting

### Inflation (CPI & PPI)
- **May 2026 CPI**: +0.4% YoY (NBS, June 10, 2026)
- **May 2026 PPI**: -1.8% YoY — deflationary pressure persists in industrial goods
- Core CPI (ex-food & energy): +0.7% YoY
- Pork prices stabilizing after spring decline; fuel prices down on global crude weakness

### Monetary Policy & Interest Rates
- **1-Year LPR**: 3.10% (unchanged since October 2025 cut)
- **5-Year LPR**: 3.60% (unchanged)
- **7-Day Reverse Repo Rate**: 1.40% (PBOC's primary policy rate)
- **RRR**: 9.0% for large banks (last cut: 50bp in January 2026)
- PBOC injected ¥300B via 1-year MLF at 2.30% in May 2026
- Market expectations: another RRR cut of 25-50bp likely in H2 2026

### Trade
- **May 2026 Exports**: +3.1% YoY (Customs, June 7, 2026)
- **May 2026 Imports**: +1.5% YoY
- Trade surplus: $82.4B
- US tariffs on Chinese EVs/semiconductors continue to weigh; ASEAN and Belt & Road exports growing faster

### Yuan (CNY)
- **USD/CNY**: ~7.22 (onshore, June 12, 2026)
- PBOC continues to manage via daily fixing and counter-cyclical factor

---

## Key Themes & A-Share Implications

1. **Policy Easing Bias**: With CPI below 1% and PPI negative, PBOC has ample room for further easing. Expect RRR cut by Q3. Supportive for A-share liquidity.

2. **Trade Friction**: US maintains elevated tariffs. Beneficiaries: import substitution plays (semiconductors, advanced manufacturing). Headwinds: export-oriented consumer goods.

3. **Property Stabilization**: Housing sales still declining but pace slowing. Government's "white list" project financing and city-level purchase relaxations providing floor.

4. **Fiscal Support**: Central government special bonds issuance accelerating in Q2 for infrastructure and tech investment. Positive for construction, new energy, AI infrastructure.
